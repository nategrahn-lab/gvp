About GVP

A family business, built on architecture and craft

Founded in the early 1990s by brothers Marvin, Peter and Erick Grahn, GVP Development has spent over three decades designing, building and renovating urban residences across Chicago's North Side.

Our Story

Designed, built and managed in-house

GVP Development designs, builds and renovates urban residences in some of the most desirable neighborhoods of Chicago, including Lake View, Lincoln Park and Roscoe Village. Our firm has built over 80 properties for discerning city dwellers, from upscale condominiums up to $1 million in value to elegant single-family homes in the $1 million to $4.5 million range.

Marvin, Peter and Erick Grahn founded GVP in the early 1990s. Collectively, they have in excess of 75 years of experience in the architecture, construction management, finance and real estate development fields.

Leadership

The Grahn Family

MG

Marvin Grahn

Architectural Design

Prior to establishing GVP, Marvin was Vice President of Development for Bellemead Development Corporation, a subsidiary of The Chubb Corporation. He spent eight years as manager of the Properties Development Division of Continental Bank, overseeing the bank's worldwide property space needs, and began his career at The Frank Klein Company, a Chicago architecture and engineering firm, where he spent eight years.

Marvin holds a Bachelor of Arts in architecture and a Master of Science in architecture from the University of Illinois, and an MBA in finance from the Kellogg Graduate School of Business at Northwestern University. He earned his Illinois Real Estate Broker license in 1982. Marvin is responsible for the architectural design of GVP's properties and manages vendor and contractor relationships.

PG

Peter Grahn

Finance & Strategy

Peter's prior career includes management consulting with Petersen Consulting, a firm specializing in the financial implications of disputes, where he served as a Senior Consultant in the Financial Group.

Peter holds a Bachelor of Arts in Economics and Real Estate Finance from the University of Illinois, and has completed coursework in real estate finance at Northwestern University. He earned his Illinois Real Estate Salesperson license in 1992. Peter manages the financial, administrative and strategic planning aspects of the firm.

EG

Erick Grahn

Construction Management

Erick is a graduate of the University of Illinois, where he obtained his Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics.

Erick has primary responsibility for the planning and day-to-day construction management of GVP's properties. He also oversees the firm's in-house team of carpenters and coordinates each client's design specification implementation from start to finish.
